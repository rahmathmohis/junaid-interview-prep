## 🔥 Quick Tips for Success

### Technical Interviews
- ✅ Understand concepts deeply, not just memorize
- ✅ Write clean, readable code with comments
- ✅ Explain your thought process while coding
- ✅ Ask clarifying questions before solving
- ✅ Test your code with edge cases

### Behavioral Interviews
- ✅ Use STAR method (Situation, Task, Action, Result)
- ✅ Prepare specific examples from projects
- ✅ Be honest about weaknesses, show improvement
- ✅ Show enthusiasm and willingness to learn

### Body Language
- ✅ Maintain eye contact (virtual or in-person)
- ✅ Sit upright, speak clearly
- ✅ Show confidence without arrogance
- ✅ Thank the interviewer at the end
